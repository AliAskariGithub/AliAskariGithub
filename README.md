# 💫 About Me:
I'm Ali Askari, an 18-year-old developer and designer based in Karachi, Pakistan. I work across two disciplines that most people keep separate: the systems-thinking world of full-stack development and agentic AI, and the visual, human-centered world of brand and graphic design. I believe the best software isn't just technically sound — it's thoughtfully designed.<br><br>I'm self-taught, having started coding at 17 and building projects before I fully understood how they worked. That same curiosity led me to found NexuGem Agency, a five-person digital studio delivering web development, brand design, and AI-powered solutions to clients across multiple countries.<br><br>🔭 Currently building agentic AI systems, full-stack web apps, and brand identities at NexuGem Agency<br>
🌱 Focused on Agentic AI, cloud architecture, and scalable systems<br>
